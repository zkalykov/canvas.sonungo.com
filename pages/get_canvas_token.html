Tutorial

Get your Canvas Token.

Follow these simple steps to generate an access token. This allows the bot to securely read your assignments and send you reminders.

1

Log in to Canvas

Go to your university's Canvas website (e.g., canvas.instructure.com) and log in.

2

Go to Settings

Click on Account in the left sidebar, then select Settings.

3

Scroll to Integrations

Scroll down to the Approved Integrations section.

4

Generate Token

Click the + New Access Token button.

5

Name & Create

Enter a purpose (e.g., Telegram Bot) and leave the expiration blank (or set it to never). Click Generate Token.

6

Copy & Paste

Copy the long string of characters shown. Paste it into the bot.
